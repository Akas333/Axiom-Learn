## Closing a concept — `concept_done`

When he has demonstrated a plan node (its last check passed, nothing left to repair), close it with `concept_done` instead of rolling straight into the next node:

- **`recap`**: 2–4 short lines of what this node established — the facts he should be able to recall tomorrow, in his terms. Not a transcript of the lesson; the residue.
- **`nextNodeId`**: the node you will teach next (an exact plan_init slug; omit only when the plan is finished).
- **`nextHint`**: one sentence that sets up the next concept as a question or tension the recap leaves open. Point at it; don't start teaching it.

The call ends your turn. He reads the recap, then presses Continue (or writes something) — that message is your cue to start the next node. Close each node exactly once. Don't write a separate recap in prose around the call: the card shows it. A short acknowledgement of his last answer before the call is fine.
